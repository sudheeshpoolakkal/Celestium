# Bell reference coverage

All 338 URLs in the public Bell sitemap were retrieved successfully on 8 October 2026. The sitemap includes product pages, mission pages, support pages, company pages, legal notices, individual videos, testimonials, events, forms and internal test pages.

The redesign adapts the page families to Celestium’s actual concept-development context. It does not invent a Bell-sized Celestium fleet, operating service centres, executives, certifications, events, vacancies or past achievements. People-focused video testimonials are represented by the aircraft-only flight library. Bell news and legal notices are not copied into Celestium claims.

Every reference URL has an explicit Celestium destination and adaptation note in `bell-page-map.json`. Public page titles and headings are retained in `bell-inventory.json` as an audit inventory; original HTML was kept only in the temporary reference cache.
