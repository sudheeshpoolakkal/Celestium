# Aerospace presentation references

Reviewed on 7 October 2026 through the public websites and their rendered browser pages.

- [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/): full-screen photographic chapters, a short mission headline, generous negative space, restrained navigation and direct outlined actions. These presentation principles informed the MALUS introduction and aircraft/medical story panels. No SpaceX artwork, copy, brand marks or business claims are used.
- [Stellar Aircraft](https://stellaraircraft.com/): a prominent aircraft presentation and direct hydrogen positioning. The [hydrogen-electric powertrain page](https://stellaraircraft.com/stellar-jet/hydrogen-electric-powertrain/) uses a large system cutaway. MALUS keeps its own native engineering diagrams and research assumptions rather than borrowing Stellar's aircraft or performance claims.

MALUS retains its curved sketch-derived M, sage/graphite palette, clear program expansion, dedicated medical mission and explicit concept-development status. New original concept images and their exact built-in imagegen prompts are documented in [ARTWORK-V2.md](ARTWORK-V2.md).
